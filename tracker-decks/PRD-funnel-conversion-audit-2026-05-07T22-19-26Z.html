PRD: Conversion Audit — in-product feature

Drop: 2026-05-07T22-19-26Z · Tier Now · Effort M–L · Triggered by Funnel Leasing "19% non-AI" claim

Problem

Funnel published a homepage stat (AI vs. 19% non-AI) with no methodology, sample, or customer name. The right defense is not to publish a competing stat — it's to ship an in-product feature that lets every Entrata customer compute and publish their own audited stat from their own data. Funnel can't follow this because (a) they don't own the funnel data end-to-end, and (b) their stat is a marketing artifact, not a product surface.

Target user

Entrata customer admins (regional VPs, marketing leads) generating board-deck and prospect-facing proof. Secondary: prospect-side procurement teams who land on the public proof pages.

Scope (in)

  • Conversion Audit dashboard at app.entrata.com/conversion-audit:
    • AI-handled vs. non-AI-handled funnel, side-by-side, with stage-level conversion rates (lead → tour → app → signed).
    • Sample size, date window, and control-group definition visible on the page (not in a footnote).
    • Methodology popover with the attribution-model spec inline.
  • Auditable methodology API: GET /api/v1/conversion-audit/methodology returns the JSON spec of the attribution model — versioned, with a content hash, so auditors and prospects can verify nothing's been changed since publication.
  • Public proof-page generator: customer one-clicks "Publish proof" → Entrata hosts a public URL at entrata.com/proof/<customer-slug>/<audit-id> with the customer's logo, the headline delta, the methodology hash, and a tamper-evident timestamp signed by Entrata.
  • Audit-log immutability: the underlying data snapshot is frozen at publish time and stored in a write-once table; re-publishing creates a new audit-id, never overwrites.

Scope (out)

  • A standalone analytics product.
  • Cross-customer benchmarking (privacy minefield).
  • Custom attribution models — ship one well-documented model first, parameterize later.

Success metric

≥25 customers publish a public proof page in the first 90 days post-GA; sales-attached usage in ≥10% of mid-funnel deal cycles within 6 months.
